Privacy

Kingston Ward Bishopric app · updated October 28, 2026

This app is a private tool for the Kingston Ward bishopric and the few people they invite. It isn’t an official app of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

What we get when you sign in

Signing in with Google shares only your name, email address and profile picture with the app. We use your email to check that you’re on the invited list. We don’t get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

What the app stores

Ward planning information the bishopric enters (calendar items, assignments and similar) is kept in Google Sheets owned by a member of the bishopric. Access is limited to the people the bishopric adds.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or share your information.
  • We don’t use ads or tracking.
  • We don’t send email except notices about app items assigned to you.

Questions or removal

Ask a member of the Kingston Ward bishopric, or email braydon.t.elliott@gmail.com. You can remove the app’s sign-in access anytime in your Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.
